# Wizard's Mob Run Support

Something broken? Something confusing? Just want to tell us the boss is too
hard? Write to us:

**[gokfurca@icloud.com](mailto:gokfurca@icloud.com)**

We read every email. It helps a lot if you include:

- The game version (bottom of the Settings screen)
- Your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version (Settings → General → About)
- What happened, and a screenshot if you can grab one

## Quick answers

### Where are my saves?

On your device, and only there. Coins, stars, unlocked levels, upgrades and
wizard colours never leave your phone. If you delete the game, the save goes
with it. The game keeps no cloud copy of its own, so delete with care. A device backup (iCloud or a computer) may still hold one.

### I want to start over.

Settings → RESET PROGRESS. It wipes coins, stars, upgrades and unlocked
levels, and the tutorial plays again. Your sound and language settings stay
as they were.

### The music or sound seems off.

There are two switches and they're separate: MUSIC and SOUND. Check both on
the pause card (while playing) and in Settings.

### What's the ENDLESS leaderboard?

If you're signed in to Apple's Game Center, your best ENDLESS stage goes to
the leaderboard so you can compare with other players. Signed out, no score is
sent.

### Something else?

Email us. The more detail the better: the level number, what you tapped,
what you expected. Specific reports get fixed fastest.
