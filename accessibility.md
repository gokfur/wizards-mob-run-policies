# Wizard's Mob Run Accessibility

We want as many people as possible to enjoy the game. This page says plainly
what the game does today and what it does not do yet. It describes version 1.0
for iPhone and iPad.

## What the game does

- **Reduce Motion.** If Reduce Motion is on in your iPhone or iPad settings,
  the game turns down its movement: no camera shake, no zoom kicks, no
  slow-motion hits, calmer doors and pop-up effects, and a still Settings
  screen. Numbers, colours and gameplay stay the same.
- **Text size.** Settings → TEXT SIZE has two choices, NORMAL and LARGE. LARGE
  makes the text about 15 percent bigger across the game. This is a setting
  inside the game. The game does not follow the larger text sizes in your
  system settings.
- **Sound, music and vibration switches.** MUSIC, SOUND EFFECTS and VIBRATION
  are three separate switches in Settings. The music and sound switches are
  also on the pause card. What happens in the game is always shown on screen
  too, so you can play with sound and vibration off.
- **Left-handed layout.** Settings → BUTTON HAND mirrors the on-screen buttons
  for left-handed play.
- **One finger.** The game is played with one finger: tap and drag. You do
  not need to pinch, shake, or use two fingers.
- **Signed numbers on gates.** Good and bad gates are not told apart by green
  and red alone. Every gate shows its number with a plus or a minus sign.
- **Ten languages.** The game comes in ten languages, and you can switch any
  time in Settings → LANGUAGE.
- **No account needed.** You can play without signing in to anything.
- **No voices and no videos.** The game has no spoken lines and no video, so
  there is nothing that would need captions.

## What the game does not do yet

We would rather be honest than overpromise.

- **VoiceOver is limited.** When VoiceOver is on, the game lets your touches
  through so you can drag to steer. But the menus, buttons and cards are drawn
  inside one game view and are not read out one by one. A player who relies on
  VoiceOver cannot play the full game yet.
- **Voice Control** has the same limit: the buttons do not have names that
  Voice Control can pick out.
- **Dark mode.** The game has its own bright look and does not change with the
  system Light and Dark setting.
- **System text size.** As said above, the game has its own two sizes only.
- **Contrast and colour.** We have measured the button and label colours, but
  not every picture and effect in the game. The red and green lights on gates
  are fairly close in brightness, so the plus and minus signs are the cue to
  rely on there.
- **Trivia moves at running speed.** Questions appear on doors while your crowd
  is running toward them, so reading and choosing happens in real time.
